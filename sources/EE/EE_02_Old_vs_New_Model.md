OLD VS NEW, IN PLAIN WORDS
  27
What changes for students sitting exams in May 2027 or later

  OLD · BEFORE NOV 2026  NEW · FROM MAY 2027

  1  Total marks  30 marks
  34 marks

  2  Structure & presentation  Removed. Now judged inside Criterion A
  Its own criterion, worth 4 marks

  3  Analysis & evaluation  Split in two: Analysis (C) and Discussion & Evaluation (D)
  One combined criterion for both

  4  Biggest criterion  Discussion & Evaluation (D), 8 marks — now the heaviest by far
  The combined analysis/evaluation criterion

  5  Reflection: how many  1 reflective statement, written after the final interview only
  3 separate written reflections, one per meeting

  6  Reflection: marks & form  4 marks, 500 words max, on the RPF
  6 marks, on the RPPF

  7  Choice of pathway  Subject-focused (1 subject) or Interdisciplinary (2 subjects). World Studies is gone
  One subject, or a separate "World Studies" option

  8  Subject guidance  One shared guide per subject group. Digital Society sits in Group 3
  A separate guide for every subject

  9  Criteria wording  Opens with the exact question examiners ask, e.g. "Does the student demonstrate
  Just a title + band descriptors, e.g. "Knowledge and Understanding," no  knowledge and understanding of the subject matter being used in their research?"
  question stated

  10  Word count  Unchanged — still 4,000 words maximum
  4,000 words maximum

WHERE THE MARKS USED TO SIT, AND WHERE THEY SIT NOW

NEW · 30  Frame A 6  Knowledge B 6  Analysis C 6  Discuss/Eval D 8  Reflect E 4
