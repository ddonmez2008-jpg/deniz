## Structure stated
24 marks. IPD (≤1,500 words) = A + B. Presentation (≤10 min) = C, D, E. About 30 hours recommended.

## Band shape as paraphrased (useful only as an approximate guide to how the bands step up)
| Criterion (as named there) | Marks | Low | Middle | Top |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A Inquiry focus | 3 | 1: focus incomplete or unclear | 2: question and example relevant; link to course only partly explained | 3: targeted inquiry; connections between question, example, concepts, content and contexts "thoroughly explained" |
| B Claims and perspectives | 6 | 1–2: mainly descriptive, incomplete or unsupported | 3–4: all three sources discussed, some justification of usefulness | 5–6: claims and perspectives discussed thoroughly; clear explanation of how every source helps answer the question |
| C Analysis and evaluation | 6 | 1–2: largely descriptive | 3–4: relevant analysis, not consistently developed or supported | 5–6: "effective and sustained reasoning" |
| D Conclusion (old naming) | 6 | 1–2: little insight; future developments merely mentioned or omitted | 3–4: adequate insight, partial discussion | 5–6: "effective, well-supported insight"; trends and developments thoroughly substantiated |
| E Communication | 3 | 1: organisation and media use limited | 2: support understanding inconsistently | 3: well organised; media used coherently |

## Points consistent with the project's main IA guides (safe to use)
- Naming a concept is not enough: explain who gains or loses power and how that shapes the inquiry.
- B is not a bibliography or three summaries. For each source: central claim and evidence, perspective or interests, what it adds, why it suits the question, limitations. Avoid mechanical OPVL; explain how origin and purpose affect the claims.
- C reasoning chain: precise claim → evidence → mechanism → stakeholder comparison → trade-offs, uncertainty and limits → judgement linked to the question.
- Predictions must rest on documented changes, policies, investments, adoption patterns or credible forecasts. "A prediction is not an emerging trend."
- E: production complexity earns nothing by itself. Labelled screenshots, interview extracts, graphs and process diagrams help; decorative images and crowded slides do not.
- Each criterion is applied only to its own component (strong video cannot rescue a weak IPD). Best-fit banding. The IA mark out of 24 is not a grade boundary.
- No universal survey sample size. A relevant stakeholder interview can beat a large convenience survey.

## Conflict to remember
Under the April 2026 model (IA_01–IA_05), the **answer to the inquiry question belongs at the end of C**, and D is a separate researched future-facing section. RevisionDojo's "D: Conclusion" reflects the older placement. When marking current-cohort work (Nov 2026 / May 2027 onward), follow IA_01–IA_05, then the official guide once it is uploaded.
